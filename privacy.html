Privacy policy

Probability Studio performs probability calculations and random sampling on your device. The current app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated network service. We do not collect your distribution parameters, calculations or samples through the app.

Theory/Live sample preference and whether you have answered the first-launch tour offer are stored locally. Appearance follows your device setting. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device; backups are managed separately.

When you tap the chart share button, Probability Studio creates the labelled chart image on your device and passes it to the iOS share sheet. The app does not upload the image itself. Any destination you choose in the share sheet may process the image under that destination’s own terms and privacy policy.
