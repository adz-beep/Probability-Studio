A little help.

Where should I start?

On first launch, choose Take the tour for four short, interactive lessons, or Not now to explore straight away. Tap ? beside the light/dark control to revisit the tour anytime. The preview has its own sample and won’t change your work in the studio.

Where are the parameters and distribution explanation?

Tap the sliders button to open Set your parameters. Enter a number or use the increment controls; Reset restores the experiment defaults. The i button beside the distribution selector explains the current distribution, its variables and an example use. You can also open this explanation from the parameter sheet.

How do I enter a precise range?

Tap the P(...) expression above the large probability. Choose the region and enter your bounds. Valid changes update the chart immediately.

Can I find a value from a probability?

Tap the large theoretical probability. Enter a percentage or decimal, then choose a lower tail, central interval or upper tail.

Why does my sample differ from theory?

Random samples vary. Small samples can differ noticeably; larger samples usually approximate the theoretical shape more closely.

Why can’t a discrete distribution match every probability?

Discrete outcomes come in steps. The calculator shows the probability attained by your selected cutoff or interval.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Calculations and sampling run on your device, with no account required.

For help, include your device, app version, distribution and parameters.
